Barbados, 9th Nov. 1816.

St. Ann's, 12th Nov. 1816.

### COMMISSARY-GENERAL'S OFFICE,
Barbados, Nov. 11, 1816.

PERSONS desirous of supplying the specified quantities of FLOUR at the undermentioned Islands, subject to the approval of a Board of Survey, and to be delivered into the Commissariat Stores at each Island free of expence to Government, will send sealed Tenders to this Office until Wednesday the 20th inst. at 10 o'clock in the morning. Tenders to express the price in Sterling Money per barrel of 196 pounds, and those approved of accepted:—

- TRINIDAD, 400 barrels,
- St. Vincent, 250
- Dominica, 200
- BARBADOS, 500

Payment to be made on production of a receipt from the Officer in charge at the respective Islands, for the quantities delivered, in Cash or Government Bills, at 4s. 8d. per dollar, at the option of the subscriber.

(Signed) DAVID ELLIOT,  
Assist.-Com.-Gen.

THE subscriber has received by the Bedford, Captain Brockman, from London, and is now opening at his store in High-street,

A very handsome assortment of DRY-GOODS, which he will sell on the most moderate terms for cash.—Being obliged to remit a considerable sum to his correspondents in England, he shall be much obliged to his friends to assist him with the amount of their respective accounts. All accounts above twelve months standing, it will not be in his power to give any longer indulgence for than the 1st day of March next. His store at Speight's-Town is supplied as usual, where orders for Funerals, &c. are promptly attended to.

He will sell a very fine chaise, lately imported, with a most excellent draught horse. He will also dispose of two very fine Negro Boys, the one about twelve and the other 14 years old, and both of them healthy and well-disposed slaves, and sold on no other account than his having no use for them.

Nov. 12—3n. HOWARD GRIFFITH.
